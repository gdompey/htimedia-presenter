# HTIMedia Presenter: User Guide

## 1. First launch

1. Install and open the app. A welcome window downloads the resource packs (Bibles, themes, backgrounds, hymns).
   You can keep working while it runs.
2. Connect the projector or TV. Open **Settings → Screens** and choose which display is the **Audience** screen and,
   if you have one, the **Stage** (confidence) screen.
3. Click **Audience** (and **Stage**) in the top-right corner to turn the screens on. A green dot means the screen is on.
   With only one display, the screens open as normal windows. This is handy for practising.

## 2. The main window

| Area | What it does |
|---|---|
| Left: **Library / Playlists** | Every song and presentation, and the service order for each Sunday |
| Centre: **Show / Edit / Bible** | Slide thumbnails (click to go live), the editor, and the Bible |
| Bottom: **Media / Audio / Props** | Backgrounds, videos, audio cues and overlays |
| Right: **Live panel** | What the audience sees now, the clear buttons, transitions, messages, timers and stage tools |

## 3. Add a song

1. Click **+** in the Library (or press **Cmd/Ctrl+N**).
2. Type the title and paste the lyrics. Put group names on their own line (`Verse 1`, `Chorus`, `Bridge`, `Tag`).
   A blank line starts a new slide.
3. Click **Create**. To repeat a chorus, write `Chorus` again with nothing under it. The app builds an
   **arrangement** that repeats the chorus without copying it.

Edit the song any time: select it, then **Edit**. In the editor you can:

- change slide text and see a live preview;
- **Split here** to break a slide at the cursor;
- add **Stage notes**, which appear on the Speaker stage layout;
- set a background for one slide;
- create arrangements (for example "Sunday" or "Short") and drag the group chips to reorder them.

**Edit as text** rewrites the whole song from plain text.

### Bring your songs from ProPresenter

- **File → Import...** (Cmd/Ctrl+I), or the **Import** button in the Library, accepts ProPresenter 4–7 files
  (`.pro`, `.pro6`, `.pro5`, `.pro4`), `.proBundle`, and `.proPlaylist`. A playlist arrives as a playlist with its
  headers, songs and placeholders.
- **File → Import ProPresenter Library Folder...** imports every song in a folder, usually
  `Documents/ProPresenter/Libraries`. Songs already in your library (same title) are skipped.
- You can also drag files straight onto the left panel.
- Text, groups, group colours, arrangements, slide notes and CCLI details come across. ProPresenter backgrounds, media
  and slide designs do not; choose an HTIMedia theme instead.

### Sermon slides from PDF or PowerPoint

Import a `.pdf`, `.pptx`, `.ppt`, `.key` or `.odp` file the same way. Every page becomes a full-screen slide. Speaker
notes from PowerPoint appear on the stage screen's Speaker layout, and the page text shows on the stage screen.
PowerPoint and Keynote files need one of these installed to convert them: **LibreOffice** (free, recommended),
Microsoft PowerPoint, or Keynote (Mac). Without any of them, the slide text is imported instead of the slide pictures.

## 4. Build the service (playlist)

1. Open **Playlists**. Use **+ New**, or click **Use** next to *Sunday Service Template*.
2. Drag songs from the Library onto the playlist. Right-click a playlist to add **headers** (Worship, Word, Offering)
   or **placeholders**.
3. Click a placeholder to fill it with a song.
4. Right-click a playlist and choose **Save as template** to reuse the structure next week.

During the service, the arrow keys move through slides. At the end of a song, **→** continues into the next playlist item.

## 5. Going live

- Click a slide thumbnail to show it. The live slide has an orange border.
- **→ ↓ Space PageDown** goes to the next slide, **← ↑ PageUp** to the previous one, and **1–9** jumps to a slide number.
- Click a group chip (Verse 1, Chorus...) above the slides to jump to that group.
- **Clear buttons:** F1 Clear All · F2 Slide · F3 Media · F4 Props · F5 Messages · F6 Audio · F7 Logo.
- **Transition:** Fade or Cut, plus the duration in seconds.

## 6. Scripture

1. Click **Bible** (Cmd/Ctrl+B).
2. Type a reference and press Enter: `John 3:16-18`, `Ps 23`, `Rom 8:28,31`, `1 Cor 13:4-7`.
   Or pick a book and chapter on the left.
3. Click translation chips (KJV, ASV, BBE, YLT) to show **several translations side by side**.
4. Click a verse to show it, then use the arrow keys to move through the passage.
5. Click **Save to Library** or **Add to Playlist** to keep the passage for later.

Switch the mode to **Search** to find verses by words.

## 7. Media, backgrounds and audio

- Drag images and videos into the Media bin, or click **Import...**.
- Click a background to show it behind the lyrics. Right-click it to make it a presentation's background,
  set it as the **logo**, add it to the playlist, or switch video looping on or off.
- Choose a background for a whole song with the **No background** menu above the slides. Choose one for a single slide
  in the editor. Themes can also carry their own background.
- **Video controls:** when a video is live, the right panel shows play/pause, restart, a scrub bar, time remaining, mute
  and volume. Use **At end** to choose what happens when the video finishes: Loop (default), Stop at end, Clear at end,
  or Next slide at end. This is ideal for a countdown video that should hand over to the first song. The same option is
  in the video's right-click menu. Video sound plays from the computer running the audience screen.
- **Audio** cues play through this computer's sound output, with play/pause, a scrub bar, stop and volume.
- **Props** sit on top of everything, for example a "LIVE" tag or a watermark. Click a prop to switch it on or off.

## 8. Messages and timers

- **Messages** (right panel) are lower-third banners. Fields in `{braces}` become inputs.
  For example, type `A12` into `Parent of {child}...` and click **Show**. `{clock}` and `{timer:Service Countdown}`
  update live.
- **Timers**: countdown, countdown to a time of day (e.g. 09:00), or stopwatch. The first running timer appears on
  the stage screen, turning yellow in the last minute and red when it is over time.

## 9. Stage screen

In the right panel, open **Stage** to choose a layout (Current & Next, Lyrics Large, Speaker, Clock & Timer) and
to send a **stage message** that only the people on stage can see.

## 10. Themes

Click **Themes** in the toolbar to change the font, size, colours, alignment, text box position, shadow, outline and
background, with a live preview. Choose a theme per presentation from the menu above the slides, and set the defaults
in **Settings → General**.

## 11. Phone remote

1. Open **Settings → Remote**, set a PIN, and tick **Enable remote control**.
2. On a phone connected to the same Wi-Fi, open the address shown, for example `http://192.168.1.20:4316`.
3. Enter the PIN. You can step slides, tap any slide to jump to it, and clear the screens.

## 12. Backups and moving computers

Everything is stored locally. **Settings → Library** shows the library folder: copy it to back up, or point
the app at a shared folder. Deleted songs go to **Recently Deleted** at the bottom of the Library, and removed media
files go to a `Removed Media` folder. Nothing is permanently deleted unless you choose to.

| | macOS | Windows |
|---|---|---|
| Library (songs, playlists, media) | `~/Library/Application Support/HTIMedia Presenter/Library` | `%APPDATA%\HTIMedia Presenter\Library` |
| Resources (Bibles, backgrounds) | `~/Library/Application Support/HTIMedia Presenter/Resources` | `%APPDATA%\HTIMedia Presenter\Resources` |
